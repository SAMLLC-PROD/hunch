# Furnace and HVAC

Our furnace is a Carrier 59SC5, installed October 2019 by Front Range Heating (303-555-0142).
Warranty: 10-year parts, registered online. Labor warranty expired 2021.

## Filter schedule
The furnace takes a 16x25x4 MERV 11 media filter. Replace every 6 months: April and October.
Spare filters are on the shelf above the water heater. Do NOT use the 1-inch filters from the
hardware store; they don't fit this cabinet.

## Before winter
- Schedule the annual tune-up in September (Front Range Heating does it for $129).
- Test the carbon monoxide detectors in the hallway and basement.
- Make sure the exhaust and intake pipes on the north side of the house are clear of snow drifts.

## Known quirks
If the furnace short-cycles, the flame sensor usually needs cleaning. The tech showed us:
turn off power, remove the sensor (one screw), clean gently with fine steel wool.
The thermostat is an ecobee; the schedule drops to 62°F overnight.
