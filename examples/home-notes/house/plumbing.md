# Plumbing

Main water shutoff: basement, behind the furnace room door, blue lever handle.
Water heater: 50 gallon gas, installed 2016. Flush sediment once a year in spring.

## Outdoor faucets (hose bibs)
The two outdoor spigots are NOT frost-free. Every fall before the first hard freeze:
1. Close the interior shutoff valves (basement ceiling, labeled "front" and "back").
2. Open the outside spigots to drain them and leave them open all winter.
3. Disconnect and store the hoses in the garage.
We had a burst pipe in 2022 because we forgot. Repair cost $1,800.

## Sprinklers
The sprinkler system must be blown out before the first freeze, usually mid-October.
Greenline Irrigation does it for $85.
