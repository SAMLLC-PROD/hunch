# Vegetable garden plan

Raised beds along the south fence. Last spring frost is usually around May 10 here,
first fall frost around October 1.
Tomatoes: Early Girl and Sungold, planted after Mother's Day. Cover them or pick green
ones when a frost is forecast. Garlic goes in mid-October for next summer.
Drip irrigation on a timer: 20 minutes every morning in July and August.
