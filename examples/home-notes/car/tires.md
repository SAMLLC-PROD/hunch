# Tires

All-season: Michelin CrossClimate2, 225/65R17. Installed at 40,000 miles.
Winter tires: Bridgestone Blizzak DM-V2 on separate steel wheels, stored in the garage.
Swap to winter tires when nights are consistently below 45°F, usually late October.
Swap back in April. Discount Tire does the swap for free since we bought both sets there.
Tire pressure: 35 psi front, 35 psi rear (door jamb sticker). Check monthly; pressure drops when it gets cold.
Rotate every 6,000 miles with the oil change.
