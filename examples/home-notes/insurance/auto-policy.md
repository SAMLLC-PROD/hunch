# Auto insurance

Carrier: Pinecrest Mutual, bundled with the home policy. Renews every September 15.
Comprehensive deductible: $500 (covers hail damage to the car, cracked windshield).
Collision deductible: $1,000.
Roadside assistance is included: call 800-555-0123, towing up to 25 miles.
Rental car coverage: $40/day up to 30 days while the car is in a covered repair.
