# Homeowners insurance

Carrier: Pinecrest Mutual, policy HO-3. Agent: Dana Ruiz, 720-555-0199.
Renews every March 1.

## Deductibles
- All perils: $1,000
- Wind and hail: 1% of dwelling coverage ($4,500). This applies to hail damage to the roof, siding and windows.

## Hail and storm claims
Take dated photos of damage before any cleanup. Report within 60 days of the storm.
The Class 4 roof gets us a 12% premium discount; keep the installation certificate
(saved in the fireproof box) in case they ask.
Water damage from a burst pipe is covered, but NOT if the house was left unheated.
Flood is not covered.
