# Biscuit (dog)

Biscuit is a 6-year-old beagle mix, about 28 lbs.
Vet: Cherry Creek Animal Clinic, Dr. Patel, 303-555-0177.
Annual checkup and vaccines every May. Monthly heartworm and flea/tick chewable on the 1st.

## Food
Two cups per day of the salmon and rice kibble, split morning and evening.
Allergic to chicken: no chicken-based treats or food.

## Boarding
When we travel, Biscuit stays at Happy Tails boarding. Book 3 weeks ahead for holidays.
They need proof of the Bordetella vaccine within the last 12 months.
